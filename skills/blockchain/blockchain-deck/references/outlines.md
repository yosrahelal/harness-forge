# Deck outlines

Slide counts are for a 20–30 minute slot. Titles below are placeholders: rewrite each as a full-sentence message.

## Committee decision (comité de pilotage, comité d'architecture, comité des risques) — 6–8 slides

1. Decision requested (one sentence, approvable as written)
2. Context and stakes: why now
3. Options studied: comparison table (cost, delay, risk, reversibility)
4. Recommended option and why
5. Main risks and their treatment
6. Plan, milestones, resources
7. Decision requested (repeat) and conditions
Annex: details, assumptions, sources

## Architecture review — 10–14 slides

1. Summary: what the system does, the key decisions, what needs validation
2. Requirements that drive the architecture (quantified NFRs)
3. Context view
4. Container view (on-chain / off-chain)
5. Contracts, standards, roles
6. Critical flow (sequence diagram)
7. Keys, custody and signing
8. Deployment and resilience (loss of a zone or site)
9. Security and compliance controls
10. Decisions (ADR index with status)
11. Risks and open questions
12. Next steps

## Audit restitution — 8–10 slides

1. Overall risk level and recommendation
2. Scope and method (what was and was not covered)
3. System overview and trust model
4. Findings by severity (chart from the actual counts)
5–7. One slide per Critical or High finding: scenario, impact, fix
8. Centralisation and operational risks
9. Remediation plan and re-test
Annex: Medium, Low and Informational findings table

## Training / acculturation — 12–20 slides

1. What you will be able to do after this session
2. The problem tokenization solves (business view)
3. Ledger, token, smart contract, wallet: one concept per slide with a concrete banking example
4. Public vs permissioned networks
5. Stablecoins, deposit tokens, tokenized funds: differences
6. Regulation in one slide (frame, not detail)
7. A complete flow end to end (sequence)
8. Risks and controls
9. Misconceptions
10. Quiz or recap, resources

## Pre-sales / client proposal — 8–12 slides

1. The client's problem in their words
2. What changes for them (outcomes, figures)
3. Proposed solution (one diagram)
4. How it works (flow)
5. Security, compliance, operations
6. References and credentials (real only)
7. Approach, phases, timeline
8. Commercial frame and next step

## Technology watch (veille) — 6–10 slides

1. Three things that changed this period and why they matter to us
2–6. One slide per topic: fact (source, date) → impact for our projects → action
7. Watch list for next period

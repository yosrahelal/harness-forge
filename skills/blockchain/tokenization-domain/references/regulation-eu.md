# EU regulatory frame

This is a map, not legal advice. Before quoting a date, a threshold or an obligation in a deliverable, verify it on EUR-Lex or the supervisor's site and cite it. Items marked **(vérifier)** are known to move.

| Text | Scope | Points that drive design |
|---|---|---|
| **MiCA** — Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 | Crypto-assets not covered by other financial legislation; issuers of ARTs and EMTs; crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) | EMT: one official currency, issued by a credit institution or e-money institution, redemption at par at any time, no interest; reserve and safeguarding rules; significant EMT/ART supervised by the EBA. Titles on ART/EMT apply since 30 June 2024, the rest since 30 December 2024; CASP transitional periods set by member states **(vérifier)** |
| **DLT Pilot Regime** — Regulation (EU) 2022/858 | Market infrastructures trading or settling tokenized financial instruments (DLT MTF, DLT SS, DLT TSS) | Eligible instruments and size thresholds, exemptions granted by the competent authority; review and possible extension under way **(vérifier)** |
| **MiFID II / CSDR / Prospectus** | Tokenized financial instruments are financial instruments | Tokenisation does not change the legal nature: a tokenized bond or fund unit stays under its regime |
| **MMF Regulation** — Regulation (EU) 2017/1131 | Money market funds | Fund types (public-debt CNAV, LVNAV, VNAV), valuation and NAV publication, liquidity requirements and tools |
| **UCITS / AIFMD** | Funds | Depositary duties, register keeping, distribution |
| **TFR** — Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 | Transfers of funds and crypto-assets | Travel rule for crypto-asset transfers between CASPs and self-hosted wallets, applicable since 30 December 2024 |
| **AML package** (AMLR, AMLA) | AML/CFT | KYC on investors and wallets, AMLA supervision phasing **(vérifier)** |
| **DORA** — Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 | ICT risk of financial entities, applicable since 17 January 2025 | ICT risk management, incident reporting, resilience testing, third-party (cloud, custody, node providers) register and contracts |
| **GDPR** | Personal data | No personal data on-chain; hashed data can remain personal data; EDPB guidelines on blockchain **(vérifier)** |
| **Basel prudential treatment of crypto-assets** (BCBS SCO60) and its EU transposition | Bank exposures to crypto-assets, including tokenized traditional assets | Classification of tokenized assets and stablecoins drives capital; calendar and amendments **(vérifier)** |

## France

- Shared electronic registration (DEEP) allows unlisted securities and fund units to be registered on a blockchain (ordonnance 2017-1674, décret 2018-1226).
- Supervisors: AMF (markets, CASPs, funds), ACPR (banks, e-money and payment institutions, AML). Former PSAN regime replaced by MiCA CASP authorisation **(vérifier the transition end date)**.

## Questions to put to legal and compliance

1. Legal qualification of the token (financial instrument, EMT, ART, deposit, other).
2. Register of record and legal finality of a transfer.
3. Who is the issuer, who is the CASP, who holds custody, which authorisations each needs.
4. Investor eligibility and transfer restrictions; allow-list on receivers.
5. Powers to freeze, seize and force transfers, and the legal basis for each.
6. Data protection impact assessment for on-chain and off-chain data.
7. Outsourcing and DORA third-party treatment of node operators, cloud and custody vendors.
